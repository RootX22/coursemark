# Coursemark privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Coursemark is a browser extension. It has no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no
user accounts.

## What it stores, and where

On your device only, in the browser's extension storage:

- the YouTube videos you chose to study as courses: video id, title, channel name, length, chapter
  list, which parts you watched, which chapters you ticked, and where you stopped;
- the notes you write, with their times in the video;
- how many seconds you studied on each day;
- if you bought Pro: the license key, the activation id Lemon Squeezy returned, the email address on
  the purchase, and when the key was last checked.

Coursemark reads the details of the video you are watching from the YouTube page itself. It does not
read your YouTube account, history, comments or anything on other websites.

Removing the extension deletes all of this. A course can also be deleted from the library page.

## What leaves your device

Only your Pro license key, and only if you enter one. It is sent to Lemon Squeezy's license API
(`api.lemonsqueezy.com`), the shop that sells Coursemark Pro, to:

- activate the key when you enter it,
- check about once a week that it is still valid (not refunded or disabled),
- release it when you click "Remove Pro from this browser".

Each request contains the key, the activation id, and a label naming the browser ("Coursemark on
Firefox"). In Firefox the extension asks for your permission before the first request. Lemon Squeezy
handles the purchase itself under its own privacy policy: <https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy>.

Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used for anything other than unlocking Pro.

## Backups

"Save backup" writes a JSON file to your computer. It goes nowhere else unless you move it.
